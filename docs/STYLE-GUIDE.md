# Nosh design language

Preserve the logo's proportions and colours, maintain clear space of at least the width of its “O”, and place it on white or Nosh Charcoal. Do not recreate or alter the logos in any way.

| Color         | Exact value | Intended role                  |
| ------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------ |
| Nosh Green    | `#62CC9B`   | Primary brand colour           |
| Deep Teal     | `#3AA58F`   | Secondary colour               |
| Nosh Charcoal | `#2E373E`   | Text and dark backgrounds      |
| Flame Coral   | `#F3764B`   | Small accents                  |
| Leaf          | `#D5C52D`   | Small accents                  |
| Cloud Grey    | `#B7BFC0`   | Muted text on dark backgrounds |

Headings use Nunito Bold, with Arial fallback. Body copy uses Nunito Sans or a system sans-serif. Language in the app should be warm and practical, without judgement or preachy about budget.

Exact brand colours do not guarantee accessible combinations. Preserve their values, choose foreground/background pairings through contrast checks, and use charcoal for readable text where necessary. Meet WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, and appropriate error announcements. Never convey meaning through colour alone. Component primitives help with accessibility but do not establish application compliance.

The product is mobile-first and responsive, assuming users may be on older devices.
